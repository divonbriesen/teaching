WEB215 • Advanced Markup and Scripting

Catalog description

This course covers the advanced programming skills required to design Internet applications and interactive web content. Emphasis is placed on the programming techniques required to develop Internet applications, interactive web content, frameworks, and using libraries.

Official description from the Combined Course Library of the North Carolina Community College System. 3 credit hours, 2 class hours and 3 lab hours. Most colleges require WEB115 first.

The step from pages to applications

This is the MERN course — MongoDB, Express, React, and Node. The first half is deliberately front-loaded with structured tutorials so you meet each piece on its own before meeting all four at once. The second half is one project: describe it, deploy a skeleton with a login, get data onto the screen, then build create, update, and delete around it.

Two habits matter more here than in WEB115. You will read a great deal of somebody else's code, and you will spend real time at the command line. Neither is optional, and both take longer than you expect the first few times.

A word about the tutorial hours

The Codecademy and freeCodeCamp modules in M02 through M07 carry hour estimates — MongoDB around five, React around six, Node around five. Those are honest estimates rather than padding. Look at the whole block early and plan for it, because they land alongside the certifications rather than instead of them.

How scoring works here

Peer-reviewed work splits two thirds to your submission and one third across two reviews, and many of the tutorial modules carry the NPR marker. The full arithmetic, including how to read a total that puzzles you, is on the Grading page.

The rules your pages must satisfy

The checker tests everything you build against the Web Standards, and your whole site against the Course Site Standards. Run it before you submit — see Validation — and work the review checklist on your own submission first.
