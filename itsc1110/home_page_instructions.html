Home Page Instructions

ITSC1110 • Introduction to Computer Science Principles

The main page of your UNC Charlotte webspace — the one at webpages.charlotte.edu/youruserid/ — is your personal home page. It's about YOU; the course site you'll build next is about the COURSE. Anyone landing on your address sees this page and nothing course-specific beyond one link.

Requirements

  • Clearly YOUR page: the title and the h1 are personal — someone should know instantly they're not on the course site. No course codes in either.
  • Exactly one link leads to your course site (the itsc1110 folder you'll build in the Course Site Instructions), with appropriate link text.
  • Nothing shared with the course site: no images, stylesheets, or scripts borrowed from your itsc1110 folder — a different design for a different purpose.
  • An embedded stylesheet does the styling. It's a single page, so there's no linked CSS file.
  • Both validators on the page — Accumulus and the Vicunadator — and every Web Standard applies. Run the checker before you submit.

Submitting

Submit the address of your home page — without index.html on the end; the server loads it automatically. Canvas carries the due date and the point value. From your home page we should be able to jump straight to your course site.
