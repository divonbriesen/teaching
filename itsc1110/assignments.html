ITSC1110 • Introduction to Computer Science Principles

Canvas carries the due dates, the point values, and the three Khan Academy join codes. The Schedule numbers the weeks of the term.

Start every piece of Khan Academy work from the assignments page of the correct course. The grade sync reads that page and nothing else — work done by browsing straight to a unit doesn't reach Canvas.

Units vary in length and difficulty, so they don't line up one to a week. A long unit may run across two or three modules, and a week may hold several short ones. Canvas shows the actual grouping on its modules page — that page is the authority on what belongs to which week, not the order units happen to appear in on Khan Academy.

Grades Take 24 To 48 Hours, and Why That Matters

Khan Academy scores don't flow into Canvas on their own. We move them by hand, and we aim to do it every 24 to 48 hours — so finishing a unit won't change your gradebook that afternoon, and it shouldn't worry you when it doesn't.

But if 48 hours pass and work you know you finished still doesn't appear, contact us straight away. The usual cause is work completed in the wrong Khan Academy course, where no sync can ever find it — and the only fix is doing that work again in the right place. The longer you wait to ask, the more of it you'll have to repeat.

Khan Academy 1 — Growth Mindset, and the Pre-test

Growth mindset activities, then the Computer Science Principles course challenge as a pre-assessment.

The pre-test carries no grade. Take it before you study anything. Its only job is to mark your starting point, so guessing well defeats the purpose and a low score costs you nothing.
