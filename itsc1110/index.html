ITSC1110 • Introduction to Computer Science Principles

Catalog description

A broad-based introduction to key concepts and principles of computer science. Exploration of seven big ideas of computing: creativity, abstraction, data, algorithms, programming, the Internet, and impact of computing.

Official description from the UNC Charlotte Catalog.

Details

  • 3 credit hours
  • Satisfies the Quantitative/Data Competency general education requirement in Mathematics and Logical Reasoning.
  • UNC Charlotte, University of North Carolina System

What to expect

This is a principles course, not a programming course with a language attached. You will write code, but the point is to understand what computing is and what it does to the world.
