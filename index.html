Shared standards

Two sets of rules apply across these courses. The Course Standards govern how you work in any course of mine, whatever the subject. The Web Standards govern what every page you build must contain, and they are what the Vicunadator checks.

Shared tools

The standards self-check validator lives at tools/standards-check.js, with its rules in standards/rules.json. Link that copy from your pages — don't duplicate it, because a copy goes stale the moment the standards change.
