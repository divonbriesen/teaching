Shared Standards

Two sets of rules apply across these courses. The Course Standards govern how you work in any course of mine, whatever the subject. The Web Standards govern what every page you build must contain, and they're what the Vicunadator checks. Everything under them — including the Introductions every course opens with — is listed on the Standards page.

When Things Happen

Module numbers on my assignments refer to week numbers, and the Schedule lists every week of 2026–27 with the breaks marked. Your LMS still owns the due dates.

Shared Tools

The standards self-check validator lives at tools/standards-check.js, with its rules in standards/rules.json. Link that copy from your pages — don't duplicate it, because a copy goes stale the moment the standards change.
