The Usual Suspects

  • A capital letter or a space in the URL. File names here are lowercase with no spaces, per the Web Standards — the same rule you're graded on.
  • A page that moved. The menus above reach everything current, and the Standards page links the whole rules cluster.
  • A course link missing its trailing slash, or pointing at a page instead of a folder.
